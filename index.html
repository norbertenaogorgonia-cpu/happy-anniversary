Hi, Meanne!

Today is Oct 31, and we're already one year together. Not exactly one year, but this is the day you reciprocated my feelings through a letter, and it made my day so much happier. I don't even know how many times I read that letter back then, and I might not be able to express how much it meant to me. Now I'm sending you this letter so you can know how much I appreciate you in my life.

I really love you so much! Even though I sometimes (or always) annoy you, I hope you understand that it's just the way I show my love for you. I truly appreciate your presence in my life. Since the day we started talking, my whole life has changed. I started wanting to be better in every way. You really became an inspiration to me. You make my days so much lighter than before just by being in them, even when you don't do anything.

page 2

I've also noticed that I'm becoming more and more dependent on you as our time together goes by. Naiisip ko nga na hindi ko na kayang mabuhay nang wala ka, haha. Ewan, ganun lang siguro kita kamahal. Syempre, si Lord ang source of life natin, pero feeling ko ang hirap mabuhay kung wala ka sa buhay ko. Parang mawawalan ng buhay ang buhay ko, ganun, hehe.

In this one year, even though hindi pa siya ganoon katagal, my love for you has still grown so big. I've learned so many things from you. I learned how to love you and how to do things I had never done before. I saw myself improve because I'm trying for you.

I hope you can feel my love, even if the things I do for you aren't that big. They're simple efforts, but they're my best. You know naman na wala akong experience sa pagkakaroon ng karelasyon.

page 3

I'm also sorry because these past few months, parang dumadalas ang tampuhan at away natin. I'm really sorry if I can't do anything about it. I want you to know na ayaw kong palagi tayong nagtatalo, nag-aaway, o nagtatampuhan. I want to build a lot of happy memories with you. I'm really sorry din dahil minsan, ako pa ang nagiging dahilan kung bakit ka nasasaktan. Ako dapat ang safe place mo, pero minsan ako pa ang nakakasakit sa'yo. I hope you forgive me, and sana mawala o humupa na ang lahat ng tampo mo sa akin.

page 4

Ayun lang. Happy One Year!! I hope and pray na hindi lang ito hanggang one year. Nawa'y tumagal pa tayo, for years, for decades. I really hope na ikaw na, ikaw na nawa ang mapangasawa ko, at ikaw ang makita ko sa altar sa araw ng kasal ko. I know we're still young and have a lot of things to experience, but I just wish, hope, and pray na ikaw na talaga.

I love youuuu!!

And Jesus loves youu so much moreee!!!!

— Norbert
